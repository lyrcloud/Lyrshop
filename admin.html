      E-commerce sales calendar

      Special shopping days run themselves: before each one, trending and new items are discounted automatically and the home page banner and slides switch to the event.

      How automatic discounts work
      • Early deals start the set number of days before the event; the event days themselves get the biggest discounts.
      • The top 25% best sellers of the last 30 days get the full maximum, other trending items 80% of it, and new arrivals (listed in the last 30 days, or the 24 newest) 60%.
      • Early deals are 70% of those amounts. Every discount is rounded to a 5% step and never goes above the event's maximum (at most 45%).
      • Discounts are taken off the seller's was price when there is one, and a seller's own deeper sale is kept. Stored prices never change, so prices go back by themselves when the event ends.
      • When two events overlap, the one that ends first is shown. Dates follow UTC days.
