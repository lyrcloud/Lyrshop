Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what Lyrshop ("we", "us") collects when you use lyrshop.com or the Lyrshop app for Android and iOS, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Questions: support@lyrshop.com.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address or mobile number, your name, and your password (stored only as a one-way hash). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google.
  • Orders. The items you buy, prices, and the name, shipping address, phone number and email you give at checkout, so we can deliver your order and handle returns.
  • Payments. Card and wallet payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Seller and partner details. If you sell on Lyrshop, apply for a business account or use our dropshipping API, we keep the business details you give us, your listings and your sales.
  • Location. We use your approximate location (country and city, taken from your internet connection) to show prices in your currency, estimate shipping and tax, and show nearby items first. If you tap "Use my location" in your cart, your device shares its location once so we can fill in your shipping country and postal code. We don't track your location in the background.
  • Activity on the store. We count how many people are viewing a product or have it in their cart, using a random visitor ID that isn't linked to your name. These counts are deleted automatically after a few days.
  • Lyrbot chats. Messages you send to our shopping assistant, Lyrbot, are used to answer you and are not saved to your account.
  • Rewards. Points, badges, gift card balances and affiliate referrals linked to your account.
  • Technical data. Standard server logs such as IP address, device and browser type, and error reports, used to keep the service secure and working.

Your cart, watchlist, recently viewed items, language, currency and location choices are saved on your device so the store remembers them between visits.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, including sending verification codes by email or SMS.
  • To process payments, fulfil and ship orders, and send order updates.
  • To show local prices, shipping, tax estimates and relevant products.
  • To run rewards, gift cards, the affiliate program and seller tools.
  • To answer your questions and prevent fraud and abuse.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

We share only what each service needs to do its job:

  • Stripe, to take payments.
  • Suppliers and fulfilment partners (such as CJ Dropshipping) and the seller of an item, who receive your name, shipping address and phone number to ship your order.
  • Twilio, to send SMS verification codes, and Google (Gmail), to send email codes and order emails.
  • Google, if you choose "Continue with Google", and for the optional page translator.
  • Anthropic, which provides the AI model behind Lyrbot and processes your chat messages to answer them.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim), which turns the location you share from your cart into a country and postal code.
  • Amazon Web Services, which hosts the store and stores our data in the United States.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect our customers and the service.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. Verification codes expire within minutes. We keep order and payment records for as long as tax, accounting and consumer-protection laws require, even after an account is deleted.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and access to customer data is limited to staff who need it.

Your choices and rights

  • You can see and change your account details under My Lyrshop › Account.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing support@lyrshop.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights under local law, and we will honour them.
  • You can turn off location sharing for the app in your phone's settings at any time.

Request account deletion

We confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days. This deletes your profile, sign-in details, reward points, badges and affiliate link. Unused gift card balances and points are lost. Order and payment records are kept only as long as the law requires, then deleted.

Children

Lyrshop is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes, we will also let you know on the site or by email.
